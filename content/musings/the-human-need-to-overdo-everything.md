title: 'The Human Need to Overdo Everything'
---
I think that a lot of this "optimization" culture that we're seeing take over
Silicon Valley and the internet is driven by a need to perfect sometime. It's in
our nature as humans to want to overdo something when we get into it. Hell, even
I do it. But Silicon Valley takes it to the point where they've optimized the
enjoyment of life out of it.

Who cares if your health is perfect? Who cares about your alcohol intake? Why
did we look at the the hellscape that is companies optimizing our office lives
to maximize some value, and go "Yeah, let's apply that to life"?

You know what my entire morning routine is? Wake up, prayer, shit, coffee,
breakfast. That's it. I want my life to be when I'm older I have stories to tell
my kids. Yeah, maybe I only ever reached 95% or even 90% of my "maximum
potentijal health biomarkers", but I will have stories I can tell my kids. I
will have lived a life worth remembering. We all get the same years and same
lives, so why not go and have some fun?
